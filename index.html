PART 2 – PRIVACY NOTICE (Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019))

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha Thailand. Contact for all privacy matters: BAPS Event Coordination Team, Email: baps.bangkok@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

From the main registrant: Full name, gender, mobile/WhatsApp number (with country code) and email address.
For additional adults you register: Name and gender; phone and email only if you choose to provide them.
For children (12 and under) you register: Name only.
Generated or technical data: A group pass ID, the date and time of registration, the number of seats reserved, and basic technical data such as IP address and browser type that your device sends to the website and its service providers.

We do not intentionally ask for health or allergy information, identity-card or passport numbers, payment details, or precise location. Please do not enter such information in free-text fields.

Sensitive data note: Registering for a BAPS religious event may reveal your religious belief, which the PDPA treats as sensitive personal data. We process this only with your explicit consent (the required checkbox in Part 3), only for running the Event and the purposes you agree to, and with extra care.
3. Why we use your data
  • Registration and pass: To record your registration, issue confirmation and your group pass, and check you in. Basis: your request to register, and your explicit consent for the sensitive-data aspect.
  • Event planning and safety: To estimate attendance, plan seating, catering and volunteers, send logistics, change or emergency messages for this Event, and reach you or your group if needed. Basis: your request to register; our legitimate interest in running a safe event; vital interests in an emergency.
  • Seating arrangements: Gender is used to arrange orderly hall seating according to tradition.
  • Statistics: To produce aggregated attendance figures (no individual names) for planning and reports.
  • Future events and updates (optional): To keep your contact details in BAPS Bangkok’s community contact list and use them for satsang purposes: inviting you to future satsang assemblies, festivals, special events, youth and children’s programmes and volunteering (seva) opportunities run by BAPS Bangkok, and sending related announcements and reminders by WhatsApp or email. We also use anonymous attendance figures to plan future events. This does not include fundraising, and your details are not shared with other organisations, unless we ask you separately. Basis: your separate, optional consent only.
  • Photos and video (optional): As described in section 6 of Part 1. Basis: your separate, optional consent.
  • Security and legal compliance: To protect the website and registrations from misuse and to comply with the law.
4. Your choices

Providing the required fields is necessary to register; without them we cannot register you. Consent for future communications and for photos/video is optional and does not affect your registration. You may withdraw any consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

5. Other people’s data and children

If you give us details of other adults, you must have told them and have their agreement. For children, you confirm that you are the parent/guardian or authorised by them. We collect only the child’s name, and we do not add children’s names to the contact list or use them for future-event invitations. Contact details you give for additional adults are used for this Event only, unless that adult separately agrees to receive future updates.

6. Who sees your data
  • Authorised BAPS Bangkok event coordinators and volunteers who need it for the Event, under confidentiality.
  • Service providers who help us run the registration: Google (Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail), and Google Maps, which is embedded on this page. The website was built and is maintained by Mozzerella, an independent web developer, who may access registration data only to operate and fix the system, under confidentiality and on our instructions.
  • The venue or authorities, only where needed for security or required by law.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with other organisations for their own marketing.

7. Storage and transfers outside Thailand

When you submit the form, your details are sent to a restricted Google Apps Script writing to a private Google Sheet owned by BAPS Bangkok. Only named coordinators signed in to their own accounts can open it. Providers may store or process data on secure servers with appropriate safeguards.

8. How long we keep it
  • Registration and pass data: until 90 days after the Event, then deleted or reduced to anonymous statistics.
  • Contact details of people who opted in to future updates: until you withdraw consent, or 24 months after your last interaction with us, whichever is earlier.
9. Security

We limit access to named coordinators, use account protections such as two-step verification, and remove access when volunteers’ roles end. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Committee within 72 hours of becoming aware and notify affected individuals where required.

10. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may: withdraw consent; access and obtain a copy of your data; request deletion or anonymisation; object to certain processing; and request correction. To exercise a right, write to baps.bangkok@gmail.com.

11. Messages and unsubscribing

Registration confirmations and Event-specific logistics are sent to everyone registered. Other updates are sent only if you opted in. Every email includes an unsubscribe option.
